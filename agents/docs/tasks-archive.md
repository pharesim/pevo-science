## BE-RESEND-VERIFICATION-SMTP-TIMING — SMTP-latency timing oracle on /api/auth/resend-verification (archived 2026-04-22)

**Disposition:** accepted as residual. No implementation. Archived at architect-unblock time with re-open conditions documented.

- Telemetry or an in-the-wild exploit report for SMTP-timing enumeration.
- A background email pipeline lands for unrelated reasons (verify-email redesign, transactional-email provider swap) — at which point shape 1/3 becomes nearly free to apply.
